UX DESIGN

Designing with people in mind.

My journey into UX design is helping me understand that good digital products are not only about how they look, but also about how people understand, use, and experience them.

MY UX JOURNEY

Learning to design with purpose

My background in frontend development introduced me to the process of turning ideas into working digital experiences. As I continued learning, I became increasingly interested in what happens before development begins.

UX design has helped me think more deeply about the people using a product, the problems they face, and how a digital solution can make their experience easier and more meaningful.

I am currently developing my UX design skills and learning how research, problem definition, user flows, wireframes, and interface design work together to create thoughtful user experiences.

MY DESIGN APPROACH

Thinking beyond the screen

I am learning to approach design as a process of understanding problems before creating solutions.

01

Understand

Learn about the problem, the users, and the context in which the product will be used.

02

Research

Explore user needs, behaviours, challenges, and opportunities through research.

03

Define

Clearly identify the problem and determine what the design should help users achieve.

04

Design

Create user flows, wireframes, and interfaces that guide users through the experience.

05

Improve

Review feedback, identify areas for improvement, and continue refining the experience.

WHAT I'M LEARNING

UX skills I'm developing

User-Centered Design

Understanding user needs and keeping users at the center of the design process.

Problem Identification

Looking closely at challenges to understand what problem a product should actually solve.

User Flows

Mapping how users move through a product to complete tasks and achieve their goals.

Wireframing

Planning the structure and layout of digital products before moving into detailed design.

Interface Design

Designing clear and visually organised interfaces that support a positive user experience.

Prototyping

Creating interactive design concepts to explore and communicate how a product may work.

MY DESIGN TOOL

Figma

I use Figma as part of my UX design learning journey to create wireframes, interface designs, and prototypes.

As I continue learning, I am developing my ability to use design tools intentionally—not just to make interfaces look good, but to communicate ideas and create better user experiences.
