ABOUT ME

My journey is built on curiosity and growth.

From Science and education to technology and design, my journey has been shaped by curiosity, continous learning, and a desire to solve meaningful problems.

MY STORY

Where it all began

My professional journey began in the world of science. I studied Chemistry and developed a strong foundation in observation, analysis, research, critical thinking, and problem-solving.

My journey also expanded into Health, Safety and Environment and education. Through teaching, I have developed strong communication skills, learned how to simplify complex ideas, and gained valuable experience working with people and understanding different learning needs

Two years ago, I discovered my passion for technology. As I watched technology transform the way people live, work, communicate, and solve problems, I became curious about how I could contribute to that transformation. That curiosity led me into web development and, eventually, UX design.

MY TECH JOURNEY

Learning, building, and growing

I began my technology journey with web development, starting with HTML and CSS and gradually building my foundation in JavaScript. Through structured training, self-learning, and hands-on projects, I have developed practical experience creating responsive web interfaces.

Building projects has helped me move beyond simply learning syntax. I enjoy understanding how things work, solving problems, and turning ideas into functional digital experiences.

My journey later expanded into UX design, where I became interested in understanding users, identifying their needs, and thinking intentionally about how people interact with digital products.

Today, I am focused on growing as a frontend developer and UX designer, continuously strengthening my technical skills while developing a deeper understanding of user-centered design.
